# Blind audit 12 protocol addendum · 2026-11-23

Owner round 24, item 1, “Withhold now, repair too.” This governs reader packets and reporting under sections 3, 5 and 8 of the [sealed protocol](../blind-audit-12-protocol-2026-11-23/PROTOCOL.md).

- A judged value flagged by the blind scan appears as `[withheld for blindness]`. The field stays in `columns_under_audit` or, for a child, in its judged values. Redundant display labels cannot reveal the withheld text.
- Readers still audit the underlying claim from its evidence. The withheld literal earns no correct credit. The lead reconciles the withheld field using independent evidence. An unsettled claim makes its parent UNRESOLVED once under the sealed record-level rule.
- Report withheld judged cells per stratum, with plant cells tallied separately. A placeholder alone does not demonstrate detection of a plant. The controller's private map identifies a plant whose substituted value is withheld for explicit handling under the plant gate.
- Header and metadata leaks remain hard failures. The controller may approve individual non-NAICS-source candidates by exact cell, matched token and full text. Every token in a value needs approval. Contact data and reserved metadata markers remain withheld.
- The public labels are repaired in a separate data lane. The draw, strata, sample sizes and decision thresholds remain as sealed.

## R13 filing years

[SCHEMA §1 R13](../../docs/SCHEMA.md) and owner round 24, item 2 require readers to check sampled bands against the cited filings. For size witnesses that state the sampled record's held band, the evidence context includes the stated filing year, a controlled source type and, when held, the public OSHA filing identifier. A missing or conflicting year remains unknown until checked against the filing. Raw claim detail, capture dates, tier effects and attribution stay private.
