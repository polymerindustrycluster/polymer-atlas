## What this says

**The route works but the yield is small.** 16 of 25 sites have a retrievable permit, 7 bind, 1 writes a process. Four of the 7 bound sites state no polymer process (a resin and chemical blender, a metals recovery plant, an inorganic catalyst plant, a plastic-parts paint-booth line); 2 more name a process with no capability destination (a roofing line's coating step, foam board cutting). **Nine of the 16 retrievable sites fail the bind, 8 on form rather than on identity** (holder form 5, blank Atlas street 2, municipality 1), and 3 of those carry a tier C term a write would take (below). The frame itself is the second limit: only 58 of 336 frame sites have an air ID.

**Expected change in the outcome line** (PIC-18 organization rows with process unknown, 322 of 794 at 393c389; only a `processes` cell on a tier C site moves it): this canary alone, **-1** (SITE-001078). Projected over the 26 new sites at the canary rate (1/25, Wilson 95% 0.7%–19.5%): about -1 organization; the 25 sites of the canary are all but one of the new frame. The three form rulings below would add at most 3 more tier C sites in this canary (SITE-000981, SITE-001282, SITE-001399) — each unread beyond its unit headings and a single term — i.e. up to 4 of 25.

- Strength: the written terms are graded `list` (a one-line unit label and a table row), so `supports_C`; no `supports_A` write.
