## Method as run
`fetch.py`: record website, then up to 5 same-registrable-domain pages chosen from the **home page's** links by link text/path (capabilities, process, what we do/manufacturing, services, industries/markets/applications, products/solutions, about, in that order), direct curl (no proxy, no sitemap guess), robots.txt per host gated every hop, 1.2 s between requests per host, one retry only after a curl transport failure, no retry on any HTTP status. Captures (raw bytes, extracted main text with line kinds, sha256) are in `S/persist/web-canary-2026-11-22/`, outside the repo. Main text = visible text minus nav, header, footer, aside, form, script and menu-like class wrappers (with a tag-only fallback when a wrapper swallowed everything; flagged in `note`).
Deviations, stated plainly:
- **Pass 2:** the first run did not follow redirects when reading robots.txt, so 5 organizations showed ROBOTS_UNREADABLE for a defect of mine; fixed and re-run (their pass-1 rows stay in `fetches.csv`, labelled pass 1). Two of the 5 are still unavailable on the corrected run (timeout; robots disallow).
- **Pass 3:** CF Extrusion's home page is a meta-refresh to `/site/` on the same domain; it was treated as a redirect hop of the record URL (target fetched, then 5 links chosen from it), so the cap of the record URL plus 5 pages holds; the thin home page row and the six pages after it are all in `fetches.csv` pass 3.
- robots.txt bodies and non-200 response bodies (403, 202) were hashed, not stored (`check.py` exempts them); readable and thin page captures are all stored. A few pages (e.g. AlphaMicron industries) were fetched under two URLs and are the same text.
- JavaScript was not executed, so script-rendered pages (SKF) are unreadable by construction.

## Candidates and verdicts (`candidates.csv`, 30 rows: 16 VERIFIED, 14 STILL_UNCERTAIN; field, term, quote, capture sha256, offset)
Each quote is a contiguous verbatim substring of one main-text line of a stored capture, 40+ characters, with a narrative (not list/table) line and the company as subject. **Reading basis.** The reading was mine, over a keyword screen (`screen.py`: lines of 40+ characters hitting a §3.1/§3.3 keyword family). The screen is a reading aid, so a statement in words it missed, or in a line under 40 characters, was not read. For the held readable organizations I displayed and read every process-regex line a second time (59 lines, no new claim) and the customer/industry-word lines for the market holds (no new claim); for the large pages (LyondellBasell, Pentair, Newell) the display was capped, so extra market terms may exist there; they cannot change an organization's count. A hold therefore means "no claim in the lines read", never absence. No second reader has seen these rows.
- Process VERIFIED: ORG-001963 MSB Machine `mold_tool_making` (1). Process rows held on owner rulings 2 and 3: LyondellBasell `polymerization`, CF Extrusion `mold_tool_making`.
- Market VERIFIED (15 term rows over 7 organizations): LyondellBasell 6 terms (agriculture, pharmaceutical_biotech, medical_healthcare_general, aerospace_defense, rail_transportation, marine), Pentair 3 (water_wastewater, agriculture, food_processing_foodservice), CF Extrusion 2 (plastics_rubber_processors, food_processing_foodservice), Chemstress 1 (chemical_mining_metals_processing), Akrochem 1 (plastics_rubber_processors), AlphaMicron 1 (automotive_unspecified), 3D Dental 1 (dental). ## What a cheaper model could do, and what needs judgement
- **Cheaper-model steps, no loss:** frame draw (`frame.py`), fetching and storing (`fetch.py`), text extraction, the keyword screen (`screen.py`), offsets and hashes (`build_candidates.py`, `check.py`). These were roughly 40% of the effort (estimate) and are deterministic; the fetch run needed two code fixes caused by my own defects (robots redirect, meta refresh), which a tested fetcher would have avoided.
- **Needs judgement:** deciding whether a sentence is the company's own claim at this organization's grain (parent page vs own, equipment maker vs processor, distributor vs fabricator); mapping words to the controlled vocabulary (polymerization, mold_tool_making, plastics_rubber_processors); deciding what to exclude from the frame. A cheaper model would likely over-fill from list items and parent pages; a screen-only run would have missed MSB's about page (the 2B lane screened this organization and found nothing).
