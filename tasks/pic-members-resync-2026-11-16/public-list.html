The Sustainable Polymers Tech Hub

The Sustainable Polymers Tech Hub seeks to advance global decarbonization through innovation and equitable economic growth by bringing new polymer technologies to market that reduce fossil fuel consumption.

Greater Akron Polymer Innovation Hub

The University of Akron, Kent State University, Bounce Innovation Hub, and the Greater Akron Chamber seek to accelerate impactful industry-driven R&D, support the commercialization of new innovations, and support workforce development efforts to fuel industry growth with regional talent. The $31.25 million award through Ohio’s Innovation Hubs Program will be matched by $10.4 million in local investment.
