# Audit 13 smoke harness

ATLAS-7 · `claimed:atlas-p1-smoke-runner 2026-11-24` · Built by: Claude Code (Sonnet 5.5). Tools only: no `data/` row changes, no draw on the PIC-18 frame.

One record per fresh short reader call, from sources fetched once by script. [PROTOCOL.md](../blind-audit-13-protocol-2026-11-24/PROTOCOL.md) says what counts; this folder is the runner.

```sh
python3 tasks/blind-audit-13-smoke-2026-11-24/check.py                                 # selftest, no frame, no model (planted cases; the count is printed)
smoke.py frame     --at SHA --out DIR                                                  # PIC-18 frame, reconciliation, exclusions, digest
smoke.py preflight --at SHA --out DIR [--families CSV] [--approved CSV]...             # whole frame, no model calls; exit 1 on any block
smoke.py labels    --at SHA --preflight DIR --out CSV                                  # label -> family evidence (git history), for the controller to approve
smoke.py draw      --at SHA --preflight DIR --secret-file PATH --out DIR --cache DIR [--approved CSV]...   # controller only; fetches the plant pool into --cache
smoke.py fetch     --draw DIR --cache DIR                                              # fetch once per source; replay receipts; packets
smoke.py run       --draw DIR --cache DIR --out DIR                                    # sealed order; stop at k = 3; cancels the rest; every call kept in OUT/reads/
smoke.py score     --run DIR                                                           # aggregates only; re-derived from OUT/reads/, exit 1 on any mismatch
```

## Reader rule: an own page counts (changed 2026-11-25, owner ruling 11:27, "Reader rule: own page counts")

**Why.** Smoke attempt 2 was void. A reader met a planted wrong street and marked it UNCONFIRMED under the grain rule that a company-level page cannot contradict a site value, although the operator's own home-page footer gave the true street at the record's locality, and the Atlas binds that street from that same page. 6 of the 9 plants had this shape, so a wrong street sourced this way could not be detected.

**What changed.**
- `READER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, the CONTRADICTED bullet, gains one rule: "An operator's own page (on its own domain) that names the operator and gives exactly one street at the record's locality speaks for the site at that locality: that street can contradict the site's street. A page that lists several locations at that locality, or labels the address as headquarters or office only, does not; a parent's headquarters street still does not contradict a plant's street." Every other rule is unchanged. sha256 of the file: `cdb6b4a7d2d69963f753369531291c4b617cdebba95f28f3da462df068399f48` (before: `8cdd959a587a4ecd055baf768ea59b0d06d222a3180a3d90de43218a94512568`).
- **Plant eligibility is a hand judgment, not automated** (owner ruling 2026-11-25, "Simplify: hand-check plants"). Two automated attempts to encode the rule in `reading.py` were refuted by review, so `reading.py` is as on `main` and its draw-time check stays the cached-page test it was. The controller decides eligibility by hand, after fetch and before any reading, and `draw --reject-plants` carries that decision into a re-draw.
- `smoke.py draw --reject-plants FILE`: a CSV `record_id,field,reason`, in the order the controller refused them. The draw skips each listed (record, field) pair when it picks plants and takes the next eligible pair in the same sealed `plantpool|P` order. The real sample, its order and the check coin do not change (the check asserts it byte for byte against a draw without the flag). The receipt lists the pairs and reasons under `plant_rejects` and the file's sha256 under `inputs.plant_rejects_sha256`. A reject that names a pair which is not a drawn plant (judged against the draw made with the rejects before it) is refused and nothing is written. `fetch` and `run` refuse a draw whose receipt has `plant_rejects` but no sha256.

**The sealed sequence.** draw, fetch, then the controller's hand-check of all 9 plants on their built packets and the reader excerpts; if any is rejected, re-draw with the same secret and `--reject-plants`, re-fetch, then run. Each re-draw lists every earlier reject in the same file.

**The eligibility test the controller applies.** A plant is ELIGIBLE only if one address block on the cited source, shown in the reader's excerpt, has all of: a name the record holds or its operator; the true value; the record's locality. And the source is the operator's own domain (the held organization website) or site-specific, and no headquarters-only or office-only label applies to that block. Anything less is rejected with the reason.

**Check.** `check.py` (70 planted cases) has four new ones: the instruction text carries the rule, and three `P` cases for `--reject-plants` (the real sample is identical and the plant moves to the next eligible pair; a non-plant reject is refused; the receipt carries the rejects and the sha256, and fetch and run refuse a draw that lacks it). On `origin/main` all four fail.

## Choices the protocol does not state (for the controller to confirm)

- **Frame.** Owners of an active A–C PIC-18 site and those sites (controller ruling). HQ-only owners are excluded by reason (218 organizations, 31 ecosystem at `6516b6cf`). `hq_county` stays a core field (ruling 15:15).
- **Builder family.** Labels resolve by family word, then the task folder's `Built by:`, then the approved CSV (`families_proposed.csv`, built by `labels` from the first commit adding each label, its PR's `agent/<harness>/` branch, else a `Co-Authored-By` trailer). Provenance covers the record, its evidence rows, every scoped child (including `host_org_id` equipment and archive links) and every `tasks/*/CLAIMS_CHECKED.csv` listing the record. `uncertain` routes primary to Astra and always gets a Hydrogen second read (forced even if Google built it: marked a routing exception whose objection is reviewed but cannot clear a flag), with no check coin. An unmapped label or no label blocks.
- **Flagged = any non-VERIFIED value, missing assessment or adverse receipt.** On the known-answer run that was every record, so nearly every record gets a second read; the 10% flag share in `sizes.py` is far too low.
- **A contradiction binds the incompatible fact.** The reader gives `source_value` (the value the source states), it must differ from the held value (a corroborating address is support), appear inside a quote that replays in order against the cached page or a replay receipt (`R#` counts as a source), and name a source that exists. The deciding quote is kept in the private results. A replayed contradiction from either read stands and is marked disputed if the other read verified; an unconfirmed claim clears only when an independent second read verified it.
- **Sealed run.** `fetch` and `run` verify the sample digest, the 40 + 3/3/3 sizes and the stop count of 3 in a frame run; every sealed item must have a packet (an explicit-list fixture may skip gated packets, listed in `skipped.json`). Dispatch is bounded to the unscored window; a failed, empty, unmetered or wrong-model call halts the run without a retry (only an unparseable answer from a healthy call is retried once); per-provider stage budgets (the `sizes.py` smoke row, `spend(40, 3)`: Astra 1.691M, Hydrogen 1.923M, Sonnet 0.535M input tokens) halt the run for repricing, and so does a **projected** overrun: before each call, actual spend plus the estimate of calls in flight plus this call's estimate (the larger of prompt bytes / 3 and the provider's mean per call so far) must fit the budget, or the run halts before the call. A failed, unmetered or empty call stops every later call at once, and so does a call whose **actual** tokens pass the budget (checked when each call settles, the last included); a run with an unscored item or a passed budget is never COMPLETE. Each packet must be its sealed row (blind ID, table, record ID, plant field) and each record's filing receipts must equal the count due, from the packet, `receipts.json` and the issued list, or the run is refused. Either reader may use the one REQUEST round (the second reader when the primary did not); a page requested by the primary is shown to the second reader too.
- **Child findings** gate only when the reader ties the contradicted child value to a core field (`core_effect`) and the quote replays. Published per-field rates count the child's own field (e.g. `registry_id`) beside the core effect, so a field with a counted contradiction never shows zero.
- **Comparison of values (A1).** A source value that is a contiguous run of the held value agrees with it (as before). For address and locality fields the comparison also runs the other way (a source that adds the city or the county word agrees), and a street is compared by `atlas.street_key` (`St` = `Street`). Names and statuses get no reverse test. A VERIFIED answer needs a cited source that loaded (an empty text supports nothing).
- **Reader output (B).** Every call writes `OUT/reads/NNNN-<blind id>-<role>-r<round>-a<attempt>.json` (0600, create-exclusive: a second run into the same directory is refused) with the raw text, the parsed answer, the normalised findings, round, provider, model and usage; the ledger line carries the file name and its sha256, and the lock line names the primary's file. The ledger opens with a `run` line (stop count, caps, the sealed order). `score` reads the verdicts only from those files and replays the run: the directory must hold exactly the files the ledger's call lines name (retries, REQUEST rounds and calls on unscored records included), each with its hash; every lock must equal its primary file; scored positions must be a prefix of the sealed order; plant detection and each record's outcome are recomputed; the gate (k, stop, invalidation) is replayed on a `Board`; tokens are re-summed; and the recorded status must be the one the replay implies, by one precedence used in `run` and in `score`: a missed plant, then the k stop, then a budget halt, then another failure (the ledger must hold a failed call or a halt line), else COMPLETE. A passed budget never changes a gate result's status; it is kept as its own fact (`halt`, `budget_exceeded`, a non-zero exit from `run`) and checked against the ledger's actual tokens. Any disagreement exits 1. A metered answer that normalisation cannot read is kept as a file (error `malformed answer`) and retried once like an unparseable one; non-string fields (and a `notes` that is not a list) are empty. Any unexpected failure while scoring is a controlled suspension (`SUSPENDED_NO_OUTPUT`, a `halt` line in the ledger): `results.json` is always written.
- **Plants (changed 2026-11-25, owner ruling 2026-11-24 20:51: "a plant passes only when the swap is named").** A plant swaps one judged field for a different record's value of the same table, in the packet only (`street_address`, `county`, `hq_county`, `legal_name`, `site_name`, `hq_city`, `city`, `zip` first, then any other judged field except `status` and `verification_tier`; the synthetic polymer-relevance key and child rows are never planted). **The script FLAG and the hand-check (owner ruling 2026-11-25 07:57, "Hand-check decides").** The script flags a plant as detected when the reader marks the planted field CONTRADICTED with a quote that replays on the cached bytes, whose `source_value` agrees with the record's true value (held privately on the plant's packet line; the reader never sees it) and which passes the witness rule below. That flag is a flag only. **The published plant result comes only from the controller's hand-check records:** `smoke.py handcheck --run DIR --list [--draw DIR]` shows, per plant read, the script flag, what the reader quoted and the draw's eligibility quote; the controller writes a CSV (`blind_id,quote,verdict,reviewer,time`; verdict `CAUGHT`, `MISSED` or `INELIGIBLE`; the quote relied on) and seals it with `smoke.py handcheck --run DIR --csv PATH`, which writes `handcheck.csv` into the run folder (0600, create-exclusive) and a ledger line with its sha256. **`score` refuses while any plant read in the run lacks a row, while the file is missing, was altered after it was sealed (hash), is not mode 0600, was replaced even with the same bytes (its inode or ctime differs from the ones recorded at creation, so score the run folder in place), or was sealed before the run's last call.** `INELIGIBLE` (the hand-check rejects a plant the draw accepted) is counted and reported apart from caught and missed. The report shows the script flag beside every verdict (`script_flag_vs_hand_check`) and never lets it decide. The run itself still suspends on a script-flag miss (it is the alarm; the controller overrules it at the hand-check). The two fixtures Codex found (a line break after `the municipal library next to`, a site-name prefix before a dash) are cases where the flag is wrong; the score depends only on the hand-check. **Eligibility, checked by script at draw time.** A plant needs a page that states the record's true value, or no reader could name the swap. The draw therefore builds, per provider, a pool of up to 40 records (sealed HMAC order `plantpool|P`; family-eligible; outside the real sample; up to four (record, field) pairs each, with a donor value that is distinct from the true one under `reading.distinct`), builds each candidate's planted packet exactly as `fetch` will, fetches its sources into `--cache`, and asks `reading.plant_reason` whether the **ideal answer** (a CONTRADICTED finding quoting the true value) would score as detected, witness test included, through `read_findings` and `plant_detected` on the excerpts the reader will see. Plants are the first 3 records per provider in sealed order with an eligible field. A record whose pages do not state the true value as that record's own (hedged, ambiguous or about another record), a planted cell that is withheld, a donor equal to the true value after normalisation and a field not on the checklist are not eligible. A pool with fewer than 3 eligible records exits with the counts and writes nothing. The counts per provider (pool records, eligible records, reason per pair) are in `draw_receipt.json` under `plant_eligibility`, and `plants_detail` lists every chosen plant with its blind ID, provider, planted and true value, source URL and **eligibility quote** (the line before and the sentence holding the true value), so the hand-check can reject a plant the filter accepted (the quote is the exact line and sentence the eligibility check accepted, from the source that passed). `handcheck --list` shows only the locked reader verdict, never an answer from before a REQUEST round. `fetch` repeats the check on its own manifest and refuses if a sealed plant no longer passes (draw-time bytes are not guaranteed to be run-time bytes). **Why this shape:** the draw used to pick plants before any page was fetched, so eligibility was uncheckable; fetching the pool inside `draw` (cached, no model call) is the least change that makes it a draw-time fact while keeping one binding draw and one sealed order. `check.py` demonstrates the scoring and eligibility rules on scripted answers; a model's real detection rate from cached excerpts is still not demonstrated (attempt 1's controls are the only evidence, and one scored as detected in error).
- **Stall watchdog** is the 1,500 s call timeout; a failed call halts the run.

## Replay coverage and NOT_INSPECTED

Receipts exist for bands from OSHA ITA and OSHA inspection filings: the cited row is located, its operator, street and city must bind, the filing must fall inside the 10-year cutoff, and the **newest binding filing** decides (an older different band under a newer supporting one matches; a newer different band contradicts). Form 5500 gets a `LEAD_ONLY` receipt. A replayer that runs and cannot settle issues an `UNRESOLVED` receipt. **A filed value with no replayer is NOT_INSPECTED** (owner ruling 2026-11-24 15:15): PPP and other bands, filing NAICS (primary and `code_assignments`), every registry class, and a NAICS value with a blank or withheld source name. It does not block, is not shown to the reader as a checklist item (the packet lists it as not inspected), and is tallied by field in `score`. **Vintage and grain (changed 2026-11-25).** The ten-year cutoff is the band-write date minus ten years. The band-write date is the date in the evidence note `backfill of D band` (1,996 of the 2,414 government-filing size-band evidence rows), else the date in the pass label of `created_by` (333), else unknown (85: `atlas-L6-corr-ca-tx`, `atlas-w16-fed-rematch`), and an unknown date makes the receipt `UNRESOLVED band_write_date_unknown` (never the snapshot date). Git blame was tried and rejected: the 2026-11-22 reshard gives 2,329 of the 2,414 rows one commit date. The newest binding filing decides across both ITA and inspection rows: every ITA or inspection row at the site's street (matched by `street_key`, bound by operator and city like any row) competes with the cited row, so a newer inspection can supersede an older ITA filing and the reverse; a filing file that cannot be searched makes the band `UNRESOLVED filing_scan_incomplete`. **An organization-grain band is judged at the organization's only site when the organization has exactly one site and it is in Ohio** (the PPP rule's single-site, Ohio-only test; a choice the protocol does not state, for the controller to confirm); any other organization-grain band is `UNRESOLVED org_grain_not_single_site`. Known limit: registry "conflicting owners" and "wrong vintage" tests do not exist.
