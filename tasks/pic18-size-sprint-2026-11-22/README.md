## Rules the matcher applies
- Street: equal `street_key` with a civic number, no PO box, no conflicting suite; city or ZIP agrees. Name: the site's own names only (site name, legal name, trade names): exact or the ruled corporate words. A recorded parent's name is `parent_name_lead`, never a held name.
- ITA: most recent year in the window with a positive count; more than one ITA establishment of the same company at the street that year -> HELD; ITA `size` code more than one band from the annual average -> HELD (none fired).
- Inspection: latest `open_date` in the window with positive `nr_in_estab`; `nr_in_estab` can be company-wide (SITE-002114: 14,000 in 2024 against ITA 80); the conflict rule below catches it.
- **Conflicts (R13 holds only when sources are more than one band apart):** a fill is held (`sources_disagree_in_window`) when any source dated on or after the cutoff at the street under a held name, whichever family, is **more than one band** from it. Two sources one band apart (adjacent bands) are not a conflict and the fill stands; the rule is the published R13 threshold, not "any disagreement holds". A rejected candidate, a Form 5500 plan included, is still a conflicting source.
- **Superseded / operator change:** a later filing (any source, later year than the fill) at the street under a name the packet does not accept holds the fill (`later_filing_other_name`); a later filing under a held name at a near-identical street or with a conflicting city/ZIP holds it (`superseded_by_later_filing`). A near-identical street (suffix, directional, unit letter) in a higher-precedence source that would give a different band holds the site. One filing cannot size two active site records (`filing_binds_two_sites`).
- Band only; no headcount in any cell. Row locators: `<zip member>|row=<1-based data-row ordinal>|id=<ITA id / OSHA activity_nr>`.
