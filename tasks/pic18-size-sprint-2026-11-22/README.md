## Rules the matcher applies
- Street: equal `street_key` with a civic number, no PO box, no conflicting suite; city or ZIP agrees. Name: the site's own names only (site name, legal name, trade names): exact or the ruled corporate words. A recorded parent's name is `parent_name_lead`, never a held name.
- ITA: most recent year in the window with a positive count; more than one ITA establishment of the same company at the street that year -> HELD; ITA `size` code more than one band from the annual average -> HELD (none fired).
- Inspection: latest `open_date` in the window with positive `nr_in_estab`; `nr_in_estab` can be company-wide (SITE-002114: 14,000 in 2024 against ITA 80); the conflict rule below catches it.
- Form 5500 (R13 source 3): only for an organization with exactly one active site in the Atlas; sponsor location or mailing street equal; plan years 2023-2025. **Fields tested:** PLAN_NAME / SF_PLAN_NAME (union, bargaining, collective, steelworker, teamster, hourly, salaried, retiree, or "local" followed by a number), COLLECTIVE_BARGAIN_IND (main form only; the 5500-SF has no such field; it is set on one of the 5 main-form fills, SITE-001599, which the plan name also catches), TYPE_PENSION_BNFT_CODE / SF_TYPE_PENSION_BNFT_CODE and TYPE_WELFARE_BNFT_CODE / SF_TYPE_WELFARE_BNFT_CODE (welfare-benefit plan with no pension feature code = benefit enrolment, not the workforce), and the plan number SPONS_DFE_PN / SF_PLAN_NUM with the EIN (more than one plan in the window -> take none). **28 of the 40 first-head Form 5500 fills survive** (7 multiple plans, 5 partial plans removed); a plan count contradicted by an in-window OSHA figure by more than one band is held by the conflict rule. This is the lane's conservative reading of R13 source 3.
- **Conflicts:** a fill is held (`sources_disagree_in_window`) when any source dated on or after the cutoff at the street under a held name, whichever family, is more than one band from it; Form 5500 plans count as sources for an organization with exactly one active site; a rejected candidate is still a conflicting source.
- **Superseded / operator change:** a later filing (any source, later year than the fill) at the street under a name the packet does not accept holds the fill (`later_filing_other_name`); a later filing under a held name at a near-identical street or with a conflicting city/ZIP holds it (`superseded_by_later_filing`). A near-identical street (suffix, directional, unit letter) in a higher-precedence source that would give a different band holds the site. One filing cannot size two active site records (`filing_binds_two_sites`).
- Band only; no headcount in any cell. Row locators: `<zip member>|row=<1-based data-row ordinal>|id=<ITA id / OSHA activity_nr / ACK_ID>`.
