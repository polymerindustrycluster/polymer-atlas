## 1. Adhesives Technology / Convergent — Orem
**Question:** does the Orem site formulate polymer-binder products?
- Own-site (Meridian Adhesives Group news, 2022-04-12, https://meridianadhesives.com/about/news/meridian-adhesives-groups-orem-manufacturing-facility-undergoes-expansion/): the Orem site "produces unique concrete and masonry surface densifiers, hardeners, sealers, coatings, and cleaning agents"; Heber production moved into Orem.
- Own-site (https://atcepoxy.com/about-convergent-concrete-technologies/): Convergent is HQ'd in Orem, its chemistry is "reactive silicate-based" (lithium/strontium/colloidal silicate); it also has "Reactive Silicon Hybrid Polymer (SiRE)" products.
- ATC's epoxy/urethane adhesives (ULTRABOND, CRACKBOND) are not said to be made in Orem (ATC lists Pompano Beach FL).
- **Reading:** Orem mixes and packages mostly inorganic silicate densifiers. Sealers, coatings and SiRE are polymer-adjacent. Own-site evidence does not show polymer-binder formulation at Orem.
- **Recommendation:** STILL_UNCERTAIN, leaning REJECT for ring 1. A ring-2 coating site is defensible only if the SiRE/sealer lines are shown to be made in Orem.
- **Settling fact:** an SDS or product page naming Orem as the place of manufacture for a polymer-based sealer or coating (e.g. a SiRE product).
