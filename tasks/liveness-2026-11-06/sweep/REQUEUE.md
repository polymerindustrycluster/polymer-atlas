## How to read this

- **Re-verification queue = NXDOMAIN + PARKED_SUSPECT + REDIRECT_CROSS_DOMAIN.** Each queued org still needs the Form 5500 check (bulk EFAST2; a sponsor filing blocks a reject only when its address is the establishment), Ohio/other SOS status, and dated primary or near-primary evidence before any status change. A dead or parked website alone changes nothing; a cross-domain redirect may be a rebrand, an acquisition (check `successor_org_id` and trade names), or just a corporate-site consolidation.
- **TIMEOUT, TLS_ERROR, BOT_WALL_SUSPECT, HTTP_4XX, HTTP_5XX and OTHER_ERROR are availability signals about the fetch route only.** They are not queued, and they are never evidence of absence or closure.
- Route caveat: outbound HTTPS goes through the agent egress proxy, which terminates TLS itself. Origin certificate problems and unreachable origins therefore surface mostly as proxy tunnel failures (OTHER_ERROR, `proxy_tunnel_5xx`) rather than TLS_ERROR; NXDOMAIN comes from the local resolver. One request per domain, homepage only (`https://<host>/`); a path in the stored website (e.g. a university sub-unit) is not fetched.
