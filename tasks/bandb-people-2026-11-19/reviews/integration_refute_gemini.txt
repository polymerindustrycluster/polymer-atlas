### 3. Domain Rule Admits Off-Institution Pages via Multi-Part ccTLDs
* **File & Line**: `build_people.py`, line 42:
  ```python
  dom = lambda u: ".".join(urlparse(u if "//" in u else "https://" + u).netloc.lower().split(":")[0].split(".")[-2:])
  ```
* **Defect**: `.split(".")[-2:]` naively extracts the last two labels of the host. For public multi-part ccTLDs (`.ac.uk`, `.edu.au`, `.edu.cn`, `.ac.jp`), `dom` truncates the host to the public suffix itself (`ac.uk`). This causes `own_domains` to treat the entire ccTLD hierarchy as the institution's own domain.
* **Failing Input**:
  - Institution: University of Oxford (`website = "https://www.ox.ac.uk"`), yielding `dom == "ac.uk"`.
  - Gate URL: Cambridge University Chemistry (`gate_a_url = "https://www.ch.cam.ac.uk/person/someone"`), which also yields `dom == "ac.uk"`.
  - Gate check on line 79 passes `dom in idoms`, admitting a Cambridge faculty page for an Oxford record.
