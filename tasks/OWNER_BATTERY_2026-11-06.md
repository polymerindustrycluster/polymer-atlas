## B-L10-ISO-TRANSITION: superseded editions during an official transition (SCHEMA R3c/R12)
Does an unexpired certificate remain recordable during a published accreditation transition after its standard is
superseded? [L10's held cases](https://github.com/polymerindustrycluster/polymer-atlas/blob/5f4f33ee3d87937611242a25ec42bcb331dcc294/tasks/markets-certs-2026-11-06/README.md#L38) include Akron Plastics,
Hudson Extrusions, ESTERLE and Design Molded; the latter certificate runs to 2028-06-01. Ferriot separately has a
future cycle start and is not currently effective on that basis.

Rechecked November 6: [ISO's record](https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html) marks 2015 withdrawn, replaced by
2026. [RvA's October 27 implementation](https://www.rva.nl/en/nieuws/transistion-iso-9001/) gives certificate
holders until 2029-09-30, with initial/recertification audits against 2015/AMD1 ending 2028-03-31. The original
GLOBAL ACI policy document was not retrieved; RvA is the published accreditation implementation witness.
These sources establish context, never a particular company's certification.

- **(a) Recommended:** permit an identity-matched, currently effective, unexpired certificate during its documented
  transition, ending at the earlier of printed expiry and the applicable official deadline. Record edition and
  expiry in evidence. Logos, edition-less text and future cycles do not qualify. ISO 14001:2015 holds in
  [L10's later packet](https://github.com/polymerindustrycluster/polymer-atlas/blob/3bd0ea3585f656dfd1f7a06396dd96d240eb1ca4/tasks/markets-certs-tier1-2026-11-06/README.md#L21) require their own transition basis;
  the ISO 9001 deadline must not be copied across standards.
- (b) Retain R3c's absolute superseded-edition exclusion, including otherwise unexpired certificates.
- (c) Hold all transition exceptions until the original policy and certifier-specific application are obtained.
