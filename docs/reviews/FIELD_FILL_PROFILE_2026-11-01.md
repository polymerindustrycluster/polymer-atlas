## Biggest gaps

Derived mechanically from the numbers in this report, oriented to cluster / economic-development use.

- **Markets served is nearly empty.** `markets_served` is filled on 7/5,773 (0.1%) site rows (all tiers, all statuses) and 7/2,009 (0.3%) Ohio site rows (7/1,041 (0.7%) PIC-12-county site rows); on organization rows it is 24/4,180 (0.6%). A cluster user cannot yet filter the base by end market.
- **Employee size is largely missing.** `employee_band_site` is informative (non-empty, not `unknown`) on 105/5,773 (1.8%) site rows, 18/2,009 (0.9%) Ohio site rows; `employee_band_ohio` on 251/4,180 (6.0%) organization rows (all tiers, all statuses). Size-based targeting (e.g. 50+ employee plants) is not possible for most of the base.
- **Capabilities (processes / materials) are thin outside the headline fields.** `processes` is filled on 3,208/5,773 (55.6%) site rows and `materials` on 2,875/5,773 (49.8%) (all tiers, all statuses); for Ohio site rows `processes` is 430/2,009 (21.4%) and `materials` 157/2,009 (7.8%), versus 2,778/3,764 (73.8%) and 2,718/3,764 (72.2%) on non-Ohio site rows. `site_capabilities.csv` holds 497 capability rows (unit: capability rows) against 5,773 site rows.
- **Coordinates and street addresses are partial.** `latitude` is filled on 4,156/5,773 (72.0%) site rows (precision of the geocoded rows: `address_interpolated`=4,156; rooftop/parcel-grade rows: 0); `street_address` on 3,638/5,773 (63.0%). Lowest-coverage states (>=50 site rows): LA 28.1% of 64, AL 50.0% of 56, TX 50.7% of 369. Ohio: latitude 1,626/2,009 (80.9%), street_address 144/2,009 (7.2%) (Ohio site rows).
- **Evidence backfill is large.** 3,491/5,773 (60.5%) site rows and 2,381/4,180 (57.0%) organization rows (all tiers, all statuses) have zero rows in `evidence.csv`; among Tier A/B records the gap is 2,230/3,986 (55.9%) site rows and 2,134/3,569 (59.8%) organization rows, i.e. high-tier claims whose evidence rows have not been written. (Match is on `record_table` + `record_id`.)
- **NAICS provenance is incomplete and often inferred.** 78 of 4,299 site rows with a `naics_primary` (1.8%) have no `naics_primary_source_type` (AGENTS.md section 4 requires one); 1,053 site rows (18.2% of all site rows) are `researcher_inferred`. On organization rows 0 of 2,684 with a `naics_primary` lack a source type.
- **Site ring and county gaps.** `ring_primary` is blank on 383 site rows (6.6%; MUST field per SCHEMA 4.5). Ohio site rows missing `county`: 2 of 2,009; Ohio site rows with `county_source` blank: 1,858 (blank is an honest unknown, but limits county-level audit).
- **Organization legal form and ownership are mostly unclassified.** `entity_type` is informative (!= `unknown`) on 1,345/4,180 (32.2%) organization rows; `ownership_type` on 1,348/4,180 (32.2%); `year_founded` on 13/4,180 (0.3%) (all tiers, all statuses); for Ohio-HQ organization rows (hq_state = OH) `entity_type` is 39/1,800 (2.2%) and `ownership_type` 12/1,800 (0.7%). Website is 3,562/4,180 (85.2%).
- **Verification depth.** 459 site rows are Tier U and 1,328 Tier C (31.0% of all site rows combined); only 983 are Tier A.
