## Reviewers, blindness and caveats

- **Locks.** Reviewer outputs matched the BOARD lock lines: A `0f3b1c9e…4b02`, B `9058060d…f0c7`. The committed `verdicts_a.csv` and `verdicts_b.csv` are LF copies with every free-text cell blanked on the 13 UEI rows, because reviewer A's cells quote SAM names and addresses (R-SAM). `locks.json` pins both the lock hashes and the committed hashes; `check.py` re-applies the redaction to the private originals when they are present.
- **Reviewer B and the UEI rows.** B's last receipt is at 2026-11-17 23:14 UTC, before the 00:00 UTC quota reset that the stored SAM response had to wait for. B's receipts log 13 SAM API calls (12 HTTP 200, 1 HTTP 429), yet every B note on these rows says "SAM record not available". The cause is not established. The controller checked B's SAM receipts: no key appears in any URL. Adjudication used the lead's stored response (both pages, hash-verified), not B's calls; reviewer A also read the stored response.
- **Navigation wording.** The reviewer instructions described `navigation_links` as "this organization's own page or feed", looser than SCHEMA §4.4's "a feed of the same newsroom". That explains B's AGREE on ProVia. The lead graded against §4.4, using the item-overlap test the earlier newsroom passes used.
- **AFFOA rows are not blind.** The two `rulings` rows (blind label `program_status`) are AFFOA records that both reviewers had already graded in blind audit 10 (A10-003). Reviewer A graded the blank `polymer_program` a field error under §4.6; the lead graded it AGREE because it is the owner's ruling of record.
- **Same family.** The lead is Claude, as is reviewer A. The lead decided from its own fresh fetches, overturned A twice (A11-073, A11-118), and used no OpenAI route. Only the band rows among the both-AGREE rows got a lead re-check.
- **Blindness is by protocol.** `frame.csv` and `sample.csv` carry PR attribution and sit in this branch; reviewers received only their blind file and the instructions, copied outside the repo.
